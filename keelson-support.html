Keelson Support

Keelson is a notes app. The developer is Jude Hathway.

Getting in touch

Email jude.hathway@gmail.com. Say which device you were on and what you did, and I will answer.

The parts

The project is the subject matter with an overarching question. Inside a project are topics, each a tab holding its cards. Each idea can be a card.

Every project has five categories: Unassimilated, Program, Crit, Tough and Junk.

Writing and filing

Double-tap empty desk space to write a card. Drag a card onto a topic's tab to file it.

Reading and taking

Press the cards behind a divider and slide up to read them all one by one. Slide it to the right or left to take it out. Let go to return to normal.

Drag a topic's tab onto empty desk space to spread its cards. Press and hold the tab to fly them home. Drag a category's label onto empty desk space to spread that category the same way.

While holding a card, drop it on the X in the bottom right to delete it. Let go anywhere else and everything goes back where it was.

Where your cards are

Keelson uses files outside the app as its memory, called the vault. The vault is a folder named Keelson in your iCloud Drive, so its files are stored on Apple's servers like the rest of your iCloud Drive and reach your other devices from there. The cards are plain text files you can open with anything. Keelson keeps the folder in order, so it is best not to rename or move the files by hand.

Two devices, one card

Keelson never copies a card. If you change the same card on two devices while one is offline and both wrote the same words, there is still one card. If you wrote different words on each device, Keelson keeps both: the card shows one version, and the other device's version stands directly behind it as a card of its own, so nothing either device wrote is lost. Nothing else is doubled.

Cards hidden from AI

Keelson lets an AI app read and arrange your cards, if you connect one. To keep a card from it, point at the card and press Command Shift H. Point at a topic's tab to hide the whole topic, or at the question card to hide the whole project. Keep iCloud Keychain on so all your devices can open them.
