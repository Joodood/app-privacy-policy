Keelson Support

Keelson is a box of index cards for your own thinking, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. Your cards are plain files in your iCloud Drive.

Getting in touch

Email: jude.hathway@gmail.com. Say which device you were on and what you did, and we will answer.

The parts

A project is one subject you are working through, with one question at its head. Inside a project are topics, each a tab holding its cards. Cards are the thing itself, one idea each.

Every project has five categories for cards not filed under a topic yet: Unassimilated, Program, Crit, Tough and Junk.

Reading and taking

Press the cards behind a divider and slide up to read them one by one. Slide to the right or left to take the card you are reading into your hand. Let go to return to normal.

Drag a topic's tab onto empty desk space to spread its cards. Press the tab to fly them home. Drag a category's label onto empty desk space to spread that category the same way.

While holding a card, drop it on the X in the bottom right to delete it. Let go anywhere else and everything goes back exactly where it was.

Where your cards are

In iCloud Drive, in a folder named Keelson. They are plain text files you can open with anything. Keelson keeps the folder in order; it is best not to rename or move the files by hand.

Two devices, one card

If you change the same card on two devices while one is offline, nothing is lost: both versions are kept, the other right behind the first.

Cards hidden from AI

Point at a card and press Command Shift H to hide it from AI. Point at a topic's tab to hide the whole topic, or at the question card to hide the whole project. Keep iCloud Keychain on so every device of yours can open them.
