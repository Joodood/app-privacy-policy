Gutterly Support

Gutterly is a comic page maker for iPhone and iPad: pick a panel layout, draw, letter, and export finished pages as images.

Quick answers

How do I draw? Tap any panel to zoom it full screen, then draw with your finger or Apple Pencil. Use the toolbar to switch pen, pencil, marker, or eraser.

How do I add text? In a zoomed panel, tap the text tool, then double-tap the box to type. Drag it anywhere — even across panel borders.

How do I add a photo? Tap the photo tool in a zoomed panel. Drag to move it, pull the corner to reframe it, double-tap to pan the photo inside its frame. Ink always draws on top.

How do I export? Use the share button — from a zoomed panel for that panel, or from a comic's toolbar for the whole book as PNGs.

Where is my data stored? Entirely on your device. Gutterly has no accounts and no cloud. Deleting the app deletes its data.
