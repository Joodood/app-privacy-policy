Keelson Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Keelson is a box of index cards for your own thinking, made by Jude Hathway. This policy says where your cards live and who can read them.

Your cards are yours

Everything you write in Keelson is stored as plain files in a folder called the vault, inside your own iCloud Drive. Apple syncs that folder between your devices under your Apple Account. Keelson has no server of its own, no account of its own, and no way to read your vault. We never see your cards.

What Keelson stores in iCloud besides the vault

To tell your other devices that something changed, Keelson writes a small record (which device, which project, and the time) into the private part of your own iCloud database. It holds no words from your cards, it is visible only to your own devices, and it is never sent anywhere else.

Cards hidden from AI

A card, topic or project you hide from AI is encrypted with a key that lives only in your iCloud Keychain. The files on disk then hold the sealed form, and only Keelson on your own devices can open them.

AI apps, only if you connect one

Keelson can let an AI app read and arrange your cards, but only if you connect that app yourself. When you do, the words of your unhidden cards are given to that app, and that app's own privacy policy governs what it does with them. Disconnect it and nothing more is shared. Keelson connects to no AI on its own.

What we do not do

Keelson has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no crash reporting to us. We collect no personal information of any kind.

Children

Keelson collects no information from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Your cards are files in your iCloud Drive. Delete the Keelson folder there and they are gone from every device. Deleting the app alone leaves your vault in place, on purpose, so nothing is lost by accident.

Changes

If this policy changes, the change appears on this page with a new date.
