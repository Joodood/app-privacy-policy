Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications published by Jude Hathway ("we," "our," or "us"), including but not limited to GigSheet and any future applications.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All data you create within our apps (such as songs, setlists, preferences, and settings) is stored locally on your device and is never sent to any server.

Third-Party Services

Our apps use Apple's StoreKit for in-app subscriptions. Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information, Apple ID, or any billing details. Apple's privacy policy governs how your payment data is handled: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks. We do not collect usage data, crash reports, or device information.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data.

Data Storage and Security

All app data is stored locally on your device using Apple's built-in storage frameworks. We have no access to this data. If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Since we collect no personal information from any user, our apps are safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
